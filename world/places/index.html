01 / Neo-Orthodox Imperium

St. Zemlya

Walk past the Cathedral of St. Michael and two golden eagles come into view above the museum entrance. Inside, chandeliers reflect in the marble floor. Alexey knows these halls well. To him, the museum makes the Imperium’s account of its past feel solid and certain.

Look closer

Episode One pauses with Alexey in front of a framed factory scene. Its machines are part of the past he was taught to remember.

02 / Great Britain

Iona

The sea opens around Iona, the island Great Britain turned to after losing London. Parliament meets in the expanded abbey. The island’s spiritual life and its new role as the seat of government occupy the same ground.

Look closer

Great Britain carries old forms into its future. You can see that in the abbey and in the dragon built by Project Morrigan.

03 / Symbiotic Collective

Tokyo

From the elevated train, Tokyo looks like one continuous city. At street level it’s divided into districts run by different syndicates, each with its own rules and security. In some of the towers, whole families live in single-room apartments.

Look closer

Follow the tracks in the skyline, then look below them. The height of the city helps explain how people can live close together and experience entirely different worlds.
