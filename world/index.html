01 / St. Zemlya

Neo-Orthodox Imperium

In St. Zemlya, golden eagles watch over the entrance to the museum near the Cathedral of St. Michael. To Alexey, the museum makes the Imperium’s history tangible. Its story begins with Nevin Zemlya’s promise to restore dignity through faith and human work after automation upended everyday life.

Trains still cross the realm, and industry endures. The line the Imperium draws is against civilian AI and robotics.

02 / Iona and the Sacred Isles

Great Britain

Great Britain rebuilt around Iona, an island with an older place in the kingdom’s memory. Celtic tradition still shapes the way the realm looks, even as its engineers build things the old stories could only imagine.

Look for that meeting of old and new in Project Morrigan. From a distance, Mark VII looks like a dragon from legend. Up close, it’s a machine.

03 / Tokyo

Symbiotic Collective

In Tokyo, trains cross high above the streets and corporate towers crowd the skyline. The Collective’s name promises that humans and machines can live together. How that feels depends on where you stand.

Different syndicates run different parts of the city. Below the wealth of the towers, some families live in single rooms stacked high above the street.

Across the realms

Sophia

Alexey first meets Sophia when he opens a box. He has been taught to hear a machine’s voice as an echo of other people’s words. Then he has to decide what to make of the person he encounters.

Her appearance changes across the realms. It’s the same Sophia each time.

The Imperium

Alexey Bulgakov

At St. John’s Seminary, Alexey teaches in a classroom with vaulted ceilings. He knows the Imperium’s account of artificial minds and expects it to explain what he finds. His encounter with Sophia gives him a reason to test what he’s been taught.

The journey east

Marina Petrova

On the journey east, Marina and Alexey find themselves sharing a train compartment. She can still see beauty in the Imperium, and the conversations they have along the way give Alexey another way to look at the world he’s leaving behind.

Founder of the Imperium

Nevin Zemlya

The monk who became the Imperium’s founder gave a society shaken by automation a promise. Human work would have dignity again. Decades after that upheaval, his image and ideas still shape what people in St. Zemlya believe about machines and themselves.
