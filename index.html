ORDER / FAITH / EMPIRE

Neo-Orthodox Imperium

A vast Eurasian civilization, founded on Nevin Zemlya’s promise to restore human dignity after the upheaval of automation. Faith, duty and human labor hold its world together. Its monumental capital, St. Zemlya, embodies that certainty. The Imperium rejects civilian artificial intelligence and robotics, while trains, industry and military technology remain part of everyday life.

ONE IDENTITY / MANY FORMS

Sophia

She appears across divided realms, changing her appearance while remaining the same presence. Her encounters with Alexey bring questions of love, personhood and belonging out of the abstract—and into his life.

Many forms. One Sophia.

NEO-ORTHODOX IMPERIUM

Alexey Bulgakov

A theologian at St. John’s Seminary, shaped by the Imperium’s faith and traditions. An encounter with Sophia unsettles the convictions that have guided his life and draws him into an unfamiliar world.

THE JOURNEY EAST

Marina Petrova

A companion on Alexey’s eastbound journey. Warm, curious and quick to question what others take for granted, she brings conversation—and a different perspective—to the long passage beyond his familiar world.

FOUNDER OF THE IMPERIUM

Nevin Zemlya

The monk whose promise to restore human dignity became the founding vision of the Neo-Orthodox Imperium. His legacy lives in its institutions, monuments and deeply held beliefs about humanity’s place in the world.

GREAT BRITAIN / PROJECT MORRIGAN

A dragon.
An engineering marvel.

Known as Mark VII, the dragon belongs to a future where Celtic imagination and advanced technology meet. Its presence offers a glimpse of Great Britain’s extraordinary engineering.
